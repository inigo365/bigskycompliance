Doing a compliance check

Emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, fire doors, water temperature

1

Enter your name once

At the top of the Checklist tab, type your name into the box. It's saved on your device and appears on every PDF as "Checked by."

2

Pick the building, then the space

The second dropdown only appears for Studios (1–10), since BR1, BR2, 64, and 30 are each treated as one single communal space.

3

Work through all four sections

Everything defaults to Pass. Only tap Fail if something's actually wrong — a note box will open so you can describe what's wrong.

Water Temperature is different: enter the hot and cold readings in °C for each location, with an optional note.

4

It saves automatically

Nothing is lost if you close the page or lose signal mid-check. It only clears if you deliberately tap "Reset this space's data" at the bottom — and that only affects the one space you're on, nothing else.

Generating PDFs

1

Tap "Generate PDFs for this space"

Confirm in the pop-up. This creates four separate PDFs — one per compliance type — all for the space you currently have open.

2

On iPhone

The share sheet opens with all four files. Choose Google Drive (or Files, AirDrop, etc.) to save them.

3

On a laptop/desktop

All four download straight to your normal Downloads folder.

Each PDF is already named clearly, e.g. Studio 3 - Fire Doors - 17 Aug 2026.pdf — so you can just drag them straight into that month's Drive folder without renaming anything.

The Maintenance List

Shared across everyone — your manager sees the same list, on any device

1

It fills itself in

Any time you generate PDFs for a space, anything marked Fail (or any water reading with a note) is automatically added to the Maintenance List — no extra step required.

2

Switch to the "Maintenance List" tab to view it

All 14 spaces are listed with a count badge — red if something's open, green "Clear" if not. Tap a space to expand it.

3

Add tracking notes

Each flagged item has an editable "Action note" box — e.g. "Engineer due 15/09." It saves as you type.

4

Mark resolved once it's fixed

This removes it from the active Maintenance List. It does not change the original checklist — that only shows Pass again once you physically re-check that item on your next visit.

"Refresh" re-checks the shared sheet for anything a teammate has added or changed. "Open Sheet" jumps straight to the raw Google Sheet if you ever want to look at or edit it directly.

Filing your PDFs

1

Keep it simple

In your shared Drive compliance folder, make a subfolder per month (e.g. "August 2026"), and a folder per space inside that. Drop the four PDFs in — the filenames already tell you what's what.
