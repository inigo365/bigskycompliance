Omega Place: your monthly checks

A short guide for the site manager. Tap any heading to open it.

Start here

Once a month you check the safety equipment at Omega Place on your phone, make PDFs of the results, and email them to facilities. It takes a few minutes. Anything that fails is passed to facilities automatically.

Open my compliance page

Tip: add the page to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.

  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

Your monthly routine

  1. Type your name in the box at the top. It is printed on your PDFs as “Checked by”.
  2. Go through each section. Tap a section to open it, then tap Pass or Fail on every item.
  3. Tap “Generate PDFs for this space” at the bottom. Choose the sections you checked, then save the PDFs to Files or Drive when your phone asks.
  4. Tap “Monthly email to facilities”, then “Copy body and open my email app”. Paste, attach the PDFs you just saved, and send.
  5. That is it. Anything you marked Fail has already been added to the Maintenance List, and facilities can see it.

Step by step, with pictures

Doing your checksSteps 1 and 2

1. The top of your page

  • Your name. Type it once.
  • Status. All clear or Needs attention, with how far through the month you are (for example ✓ Done for Nov).
  • A section. Tap to open it. A red dot means something in it is marked Fail.
  • Generate PDFs for this space. Always at the bottom.
  • Monthly email to facilities. Writes the email for you.

2. Pass or Fail

Everything starts on Pass, so you only touch the things that are not right. If something fails, tap Fail and say what is wrong.

  • Pass / Fail. Tapped Fail by mistake? Tap Pass and it clears.
  • What’s wrong. Say it plainly, e.g. “Light not coming on”. Facilities read this.
  • Action taken. What you are doing about it, e.g. “Reported to facilities”.
  • Fixed on the spot. Tick this only if you put it right straight away. It stays on your PDF but is not sent to facilities as a fault.
Fire Doors and Water Temperature show “No checkpoints added yet”. That just means nothing has been set up there. Skip them, and only generate PDFs for the sections you actually checked. If something is missing from a list, email facilities@bigskylondon.com and it will be added.
Making the PDFsStep 3
  • Tick the sections you checked. Leave any you did not do unticked.
  • Generate. Then save the PDFs to Files or Drive when your phone opens the share sheet.

Don’t worry if you have no signal when you generate. The app keeps the update and sends it when you are back online.

Sending your emailStep 4

This tells facilities which checks are done, says everything is clear unless it is listed, and lists anything still open with the issue in bold and the action note under it. A check you have not done yet is named at the top. Generate your PDFs first, so you have them to attach.

  • Month. This month by default.
  • Copy body and open my email app. Starts a new email to facilities@bigskylondon.com, with the subject filled in, and copies the message.
  • Copy email body. Just copies it, if you would rather write the email yourself.
  1. Tap Copy body and open my email app.
  2. Paste the message into the email (press and hold, then Paste).
  3. Attach the PDFs you saved.
  4. Check the To address is facilities@bigskylondon.com, and send.
What happens to faultsStep 5

Everything you mark Fail goes onto the shared Maintenance List when you generate your PDFs, so you do not have to report it separately. Tap the Maintenance List tab to see it.

  • Your site and how many faults are open.
  • Action note. Add an update, e.g. “Electrician booked for Friday”.
  • Mark resolved. Facilities normally do this once it is fixed.

You do not need to do anything with the list unless you want to add a note.

Quick answers
How often do I do this?
Once a month.
I tapped Fail by mistake.
Tap Pass on that item. It clears.
I fixed it myself straight away.
Tap Fail, say what was wrong and what you did, and tick Fixed on the spot. It stays on the PDF but is not sent to facilities as an open fault.
I added a note and want to change it.
Edit the box and generate the PDFs again. Facilities see the latest wording.
I have no signal.
If the page is already open, carry on as normal. It sends the update when you are back online.
I forgot to type my name.
Type it, then generate the PDFs again.
The page looks different or has stopped working.
Close it and open it again. If it still does not work, email facilities.
Something is missing from my list.
Email facilities with what it is and where it is, and it will be added.
Stuck? Email facilities@bigskylondon.com.
